Cryptographic Custody Lifecycle

Every state transition is notarized by smart contracts on Ethereum, preventing retroactive tampering.

01

Registration

User submits device payload. Smart contract invokes registerEWaste() and generates the genesis hash.

02

Intake Drop-off

Authorized collector verifies physical hardware and calls collectEWaste() to assume legal responsibility.

03

Logistics Transit

Custody transfers to transit. Status updates to IN TRANSIT with courier wallet signature.

04

Plant Receipt

Recycling plant logs warehouse intake via receiveEWaste(), preparing batches for smelting and separation.

05

Certified Recovery

Plant logs reclaimed grams of Copper, Plastic, and Gold. completeRecycling() permanently seals the asset.

Evaluating EcoChain against standard centralized scrap management databases.
